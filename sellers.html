Good to know

  • "Likely," not "for sure." These are owners who fit the patterns of people who tend to sell. It tells Josh where to spend his time, not who is definitely moving.
  • Sales are recorded through the state's latest update. A home that sold after that may still show the old owner, so check the MLS before reaching out. The page pulls the newest public records every time a town is picked.
  • No owner names. New Jersey leaves names out of its free public file. For landlords, the mailing address is where their tax bill goes, which is the best place to send a letter or postcard.
  • Best uses: postcards and letters to landlords, door hangers on streets with lots of move-up or equity homes, and knowing which doors to knock on around a new listing.
  • Phone calls: check the Do Not Call list before calling anyone you don't already know.
  • Works for any town in New Jersey. Needs an internet connection. The checkmarks you tick are saved on this device only.
  • Source: NJ Office of GIS statewide parcels with MOD-IV tax records (public).
